Why LingoWorld?

Big translators cover the famous languages of the world — and LingoWorld covers those too, 100+ of them, from English and Chinese to Arabic, French, Spanish and Urdu. But one language people write every day was missing everywhere: Roman Urdu, the Urdu written in English letters on every phone in Pakistan. LingoWorld has it built in as a special mode.
